OFFICIAL USE & CORRECTIONS

Official use and corrections.

A clear enquiry gives the archive the context needed to review a published record or a proposed use of the name.

Corrections to a published record

If you have found an error or omission in material published here, include the item concerned, the source or point in question, and where the material appears. Additions and corrections are considered with accuracy, references, and context in mind.

Official forms and proposed use

For questions about the official form of the name Aquira, publication, or proposed use, please include the material, intended placement, purpose, and intended date. Aquira1978 will review the enquiry and direct it to the appropriate contact where needed.

An enquiry alone does not grant permission for use. This page is an administrative guide and does not provide legal advice or a licence.

Contact the archive

Send the relevant material and context to aquirae@me.com. For works, artist information, collaborations, or licensing, please use the official artist site instead.
