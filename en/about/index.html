ARCHIVE PRINCIPLES

About the official Aquira archive.

At Aquira1978, our priority is not to increase information but to preserve records with their references and context intact.

Standards for the record

We include only content for which publicly available primary materials, date of creation and references can be verified. Information whose facts are still being verified is not published.

Approach to updates

When records are added or corrected, accuracy takes priority. We value records with sources and context over fragmentary information.
