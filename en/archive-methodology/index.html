ARCHIVE METHODOLOGY

How the Aquira archive is recorded.

A public archive should make its sources and limits as clear as its published material.

What can be included

Material is included only when publicly available primary material, its creation date, and a reference can be verified. Where it can be made public, the record keeps the date, context, and source together.

What remains unpublished

Conjecture, incomplete information, and facts still under verification are not presented as official records. A concise, source-led record is more useful than a complete-looking account that cannot be checked.

How a record changes

When material is added or corrected, accuracy takes priority. Questions about a published source, a correction, or an official form of the name can be sent through the contact channel for review.
