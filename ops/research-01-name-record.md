## Scope and editorial conclusion

This page is an official origin-and-archive record for the name **Aquira**. It should explain the role of Aquira1978, the evidence standard used for public records, and the deliberate absence of a dated history until a suitable primary source is available. It must not become a biography, legal-advice page, permissions notice, or substitute for the works/artist and public-practice sites.

The reviewed public sources establish the site’s stated role and method, but do not establish the linguistic meaning or etymology of “Aquira,” the historical significance of “1978,” a first-use date, or a dated sequence of name-related events. Accordingly, the timeline for this page should have **zero entries** at launch. This is an evidence decision, not a claim that no historical records exist.

## Safe factual claims

1. **Aquira1978 identifies itself as the official English-language archive for the Aquira name, its origin, and its verified record.** This describes the site’s public role and is not an independent legal conclusion.
2. **The Japanese site describes Aquira1978 as an official site handling records concerning the name’s origin, history, archive, and brand use.**
3. **The archive organizes records about the background to the name and activities when verifiable sources are available.** Conjecture and unverified information are not treated as official records.
4. **The stated inclusion standard is publicly available primary material, a creation date, and references that can be verified.**
5. **Information whose facts are still being verified is not published as an established official record.**
6. **Where material can be made public, the archive adds dates, context, and references so it can be consulted later; accuracy and sourced context take priority over fragmentary information.**
7. **The official information roles are intentionally distinct:** `aquira1978.com` covers the name’s origin, history, and archive; `aquira.art` covers works and artist information; `aquira.org` covers dialogue, collaboration, and projects engaging with society.
8. **The archive welcomes enquiries about corrections to published material, official forms of the name, and the name or records.** An enquiry is not a grant of permission or licence.
9. **No dated timeline entries are publishable from the reviewed sources at present.** The sources provide a method and site-division statements, not a date-and-context-backed historical sequence.

## English public-facing page copy

# Aquira Name Record

## The official record for the name

Aquira1978 is the official origin-and-archive site for the Aquira name. It preserves publicly verifiable records with their dates, references, and context, rather than filling gaps with conjecture.

## A source-led record

Material is added only when a publicly available primary source, its creation date, and a reference can be checked. Information still being verified is not presented as an established record. When a suitable public source is available, the record can be updated with the relevant date, context, and citation.

## No dated history published yet

No dated timeline entries are published here at present. The reviewed public sources establish the archive’s role and evidence standard, but do not provide a verified date-and-context sequence for the origin or history of the name. This empty state is intentional and will not be filled with guesses.

## Keep the official information roles distinct

Aquira1978 records the name’s origin, history, and archive. For works and artist information, visit [aquira.art](https://www.aquira.art/en/). For dialogue, collaborations, and projects engaging with society, visit [aquira.org](https://www.aquira.org/en/). Questions about a published record, a correction, or an official form of the name may be sent through the [Aquira1978 contact page](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/contact/). An enquiry alone does not grant permission for use.
