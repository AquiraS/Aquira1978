# Research Note: Archive Methodology

**Page scope:** `/en/archive-methodology/` and `/archive-methodology/`
**Research date:** 2026-08-31
**Editorial status:** Safe for public-facing draft copy; no historical timeline entries are proposed.

## Editorial conclusion

Aquira1978 can publish a concise methodology page explaining what the archive records and the minimum evidence standard it applies. The public record supports a source-led approach: include only material for which a publicly available primary source, creation date, and reference can be verified; do not publish information still under factual review; and prefer records with context over fragments. The page should state that Aquira1978 is the origin-and-archive site, while works and artist information belong on aquira.art and dialogue, collaboration, and projects belong on aquira.org.

No timeline entries are recommended. The reviewed official sources state a methodology and site division but do not provide a dated, independently documented sequence suitable for a public timeline on this page.

## Safe factual claims

1. **Aquira1978 is the official English-language archive for the Aquira name, its origin, and its verified record.** This is the wording on the English homepage; the Japanese homepage describes the site as handling records concerning the name’s origin, history, archive, and brand use.
2. **The archive records the background to the Aquira name and activities when verifiable sources are available.** The official About page says it organises records with verifiable sources and does not treat conjecture or unverified information as an official record.
3. **Inclusion requires publicly available primary materials, a creation date, and references that can be verified.** This is an explicit standard on both English and Japanese About pages.
4. **Information whose facts are still being verified is not published as an established archive record.** This is stated directly on the About page.
5. **When records are added or corrected, accuracy takes priority.** The official methodology text says the archive values sourced, contextual records over fragmentary information.
6. **Where material can be made public, the archive adds dates, context, and references so it can be consulted later.** This is stated on the English homepage.
7. **The site roles are intentionally distinct:** aquira1978.com covers name origin, history, and archive; aquira.art covers works and the artist profile (and its own official information concerning collaborations and licensing); aquira.org covers dialogue, collaboration, and projects engaging with society. The archive page should link outward rather than duplicate those subjects.
8. **The archive welcomes enquiries about corrections to published material, official forms of the name, and the name or records.** This is supported by the English homepage/About page. It does not constitute a promise of permission, licensing, ownership, or legal advice.

## English public-facing page copy

# Archive Methodology

## A source-led record

Aquira1978 is the official origin-and-archive site for the Aquira name. We preserve records with their references and context intact, rather than adding information for its own sake. This page explains the standard used for the public archive.

## What can be included

We include material only when publicly available primary materials, a creation date, and references can be verified. Where material can be made public, we record its date, context, and source so that it can be consulted later. Conjecture and information whose facts are still being verified are not presented as official records.

## How records are updated

When a record is added or corrected, accuracy takes priority. We value a sourced and contextual record over a fragment that cannot be checked. Questions about corrections, official forms of the name, or published records may be sent through the contact channel; an enquiry is not a grant of permission or licence.

## Keeping the network distinct

Aquira1978 covers the name’s origin, history, and archive. For works and the artist profile, visit [aquira.art](https://www.aquira.art/en/). For dialogue, collaborations, and projects engaging with society, visit [aquira.org](https://www.aquira.org/en/). No timeline is presented here unless a dated sequence can be supported by appropriate primary sources.

## Japanese public-facing equivalent

# アーカイブの記録方法

## 出典をもとに記録する

Aquira1978は、Aquiraという名称の起点と記録を扱う公式サイトです。情報を増やすこと自体ではなく、参照先と文脈を保った記録を残すことを大切にしています。ここでは、公開アーカイブに掲載する内容の基準を示します。

## 掲載できる内容

掲載するのは、公開済みの一次資料、作成日、参照先を確認できる内容に限ります。公開できる場合は、後から確認できるよう、時点・文脈・出典を添えて記録します。推測や、事実確認中の情報は、公式な記録として掲載しません。

## 記録の更新

記録の追加や訂正では、内容の正確さを優先します。確認できない断片的な情報よりも、出典と文脈のある記録を大切にします。掲載内容の訂正、Aquiraの公式表記、名称や記録についてのお問い合わせは、窓口からお知らせください。お問い合わせによって、利用許諾やライセンスが成立するものではありません。

## 公式サイトの役割を分ける

Aquira1978は、名称の起点・来歴・アーカイブを扱います。作品・作家情報は[aquira.art](https://www.aquira.art/)、対話・協働・社会と交わるプロジェクトは[aquira.org](https://www.aquira.org/)をご覧ください。適切な一次資料にもとづく日付のある記録が確認できない限り、このページに年表は掲載しません。

## Timeline decision

**No entries.** The available official sources establish the archive standard and network roles, but do not supply a documented date-and-context sequence that should be presented as a timeline. This is an intentional omission, not a claim that no historical events exist.

## Facts to exclude pending a primary source

- The linguistic etymology, translation, pronunciation history, or meaning of “Aquira.”
- Any assertion that “1978” is a founding year, birth year, creation date, first use, or other historical milestone.
- A chronology of the name, activities, publications, exhibitions, projects, or collaborations; no suitable primary-source sequence was found in this review.
- Artist biography, education, residence history beyond what another official site specifically publishes, exhibitions, awards, clients, collections, or career milestones; these belong on aquira.art and require their own sources.
- Ownership, authorship, trademark status, registration, licensing terms, permissions, exclusivity, or legal conclusions concerning the name, brand, works, or domains.
- Claims that the archive is complete, exhaustive, independent, or permanently preserved.
- Undated News-page material as evidence of origin or historical record; the reviewed News landing page was not directly relevant to this methodology page.
- Any statement that an enquiry, contact form, or inclusion in the archive grants permission to use the name or works.
- Unverified plans, outcomes, dates, locations, or participant claims concerning public projects; those are the subject of aquira.org’s project records and require verifiable documentation.

## Citation and implementation note

The copy above is intentionally narrower than a biography or legal policy. It mirrors the existing official language and preserves the division of information across the three domains. If the page later adds examples or a timeline, each item should display an appropriate public primary source, creation/publication date, and context before publication.
