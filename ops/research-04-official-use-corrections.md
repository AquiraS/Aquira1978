## Scope and editorial rule

This page should explain how to submit a correction or an enquiry about the official form or publication/use of the name Aquira. It is not a legal-advice page, a licence, a permission notice, or a biography. The copy below makes only claims already stated on the official public sites. It does not infer an etymology, ownership, legal status, historical date, permission, or event.

The supplied SEO strategy specifically defines Aquira1978 as an “official name, origin, and archive record,” directs works and artist information to `aquira.art`, and directs public-practice material to `aquira.org`. It also requires a primary source, date, and context before a new historical record or timeline entry is published. [1]

## Proposed English public-facing copy

# Official Use & Corrections

## Corrections to the record

If you have found an error or omission in material published by Aquira1978, please contact the official archive. To help us check the record, include the material concerned, the source or point you are querying, where it appears, and—where relevant—the intended date or context. Additions and corrections are considered with accuracy, references, and context in mind. Information that is still being verified is not published as an official record.

## Official forms and proposed use

For questions about the official form of the name Aquira, or about publication and proposed use, please describe the material, where it will appear, its purpose, and the intended date. Aquira1978 will review the enquiry and direct it to the appropriate contact where needed. **An enquiry alone does not grant permission for use.** This page does not provide legal advice or constitute a licence.

## Which site to contact

Aquira1978 records the name’s origin, history, archive, and brand use. For works, the artist profile, collaborations, or licensing, visit [aquira.art](https://www.aquira.art/en/). For dialogue, collaboration, and projects engaging with society, visit [aquira.org](https://www.aquira.org/en/).

## Contact

Send the material and context to [aquirae@me.com](mailto:aquirae@me.com). Please do not send confidential material unless the appropriate arrangements have been confirmed.
