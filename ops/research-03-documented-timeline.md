# Research note — Documented Timeline

**Proposed routes:** `/en/documented-timeline/` and `/documented-timeline/`
**Research date:** 2026-08-31
**Editorial decision:** Publish the page as a source standard and an intentionally empty timeline. No dated timeline entries are supported by the reviewed public primary materials.

## Scope and decision

Aquira1978 is the official origin-and-archive record. Its public homepage says that it handles records concerning the Aquira name’s origin, history, archive, and brand use, while works and artist information belong on `aquira.art` and dialogue/collaborative projects belong on `aquira.org` ([Aquira1978 Japanese homepage](https://www.aquira1978.com/); [Aquira1978 English homepage](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/)). The supplied SEO strategy explicitly says that a dated timeline should be created only when an original source, date, and context suitable for public record are available, and that unsupported biography, etymology, exhibitions, collections, ownership, or licensing claims must not be added ([US-English SEO Metadata and Keyword-Structure Plan](../US_ENGLISH_SEO_METADATA_PLAN.md), especially lines 24 and 43–45).

The reviewed official pages provide archive principles and current site metadata, but do not provide a primary source establishing a historical event/date for the Aquira name. Therefore this page should contain **no timeline entries at launch**. This is a deliberate evidence decision, not an omission to be filled with inference.

## Safe factual claims

1. **Aquira1978 is the official origin-and-archive site for the Aquira name and its record.** The English homepage describes it as “the official English-language archive for the Aquira name, its origin, and its verified record.” Source: [https://www.aquira1978.com/en/](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/).
2. **The archive publishes only material that can be checked against publicly available primary materials, a creation date, and references.** Source: [https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/).
3. **Information still being verified is not published as an established record.** Source: [https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/).
4. **Public records should be presented with dates, context, and references when they can be made public.** Source: [https://www.aquira1978.com/en/](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/).
5. **At the time of review, no dated historical timeline entries could be verified from the reviewed official public sources.** This supports an empty-state page; it is not a claim that no such records exist anywhere.
6. **The information roles of the official network are distinct:** `aquira.art` covers works and artist information; `aquira.org` covers dialogue, collaborations, and projects engaging with society; `aquira1978.com` covers name origin/history/archive. Sources: [https://www.aquira.art/en/](https://www.aquira.art/en/), [https://www.aquira.org/en/](https://www.aquira.org/en/), and [https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/).
7. **The pages reviewed display “Last updated 2026-08-31” / “Official information last updated: 2026-08-31.”** This is a site-maintenance date, not a historical date for the Aquira name. Sources: [Aquira1978 English homepage](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/), [Aquira1978 About](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/about/), [Aquira art homepage](https://www.aquira.art/en/), and [Aquira.org homepage](https://www.aquira.org/en/).

## Proposed English public-facing copy

# Documented Timeline

## A record only when the source can be checked

Aquira1978 preserves records concerning the origin and history of the Aquira name. A timeline entry is added only when a publicly available primary source, its date, and its context can be verified. Information still being checked is not presented as an established record.

## No dated entries published yet

No historical timeline entries are published here at present. This page will be updated when a suitable public source and the necessary date and context are ready for citation. The absence of entries is intentional: the archive does not fill gaps with guesses or unverified accounts.

## Keep the official information roles distinct

For works and artist information, visit [aquira.art](https://www.aquira.art/en/). For dialogue, collaborations, and projects engaging with society, visit [aquira.org](https://www.aquira.org/en/). This site remains the record for the Aquira name’s origin, history, and archive.

## Source and correction enquiries

Questions about a published source, a correction, or an official form of the name may be sent through the [Aquira1978 contact page](https://www.aquira1978.com/en/contact/). Contact is an enquiry route only; it is not a statement that permission, ownership, or licensing has been granted.

## Facts to exclude pending a primary source

* The meaning, linguistic etymology, naming story, or any interpretation of “Aquira.”
* Any claim that “1978” is the year the name, person, practice, business, or archive began; the domain/brand string alone is not evidence.
* A first-use date, adoption date, legal registration date, trademark status, ownership, authorship, or licensing/permission claim.
* Unsourced biography, identity claims, nationality, education, career chronology, or a claim that the art-site profile proves the name’s historical origin.
* Exhibitions, awards, collections, commissions, collaborations, projects, locations, or publication dates imported from `aquira.art`, `aquira.org`, or News without a specific primary source and relevance to the name/history record.
* Treating the 2026-08-31 page-update labels as historical timeline events.
* Treating article-list timestamps, membership/store listings, search snippets, or third-party links on the News profile as verified historical events without opening a specific source and checking its date, provenance, and context.
* Claims that contacting Aquira1978 grants permission or establishes ownership, endorsement, or legal rights.
